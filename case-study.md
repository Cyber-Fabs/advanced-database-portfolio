# Case Study: CareBridge Specialist Clinic, Abuja (simulated organisation)

**Nature of the business:** A mid-sized outpatient clinic offering general consultations, specialist services (cardiology, paediatrics, obstetrics), an on-site laboratory and a pharmacy. It serves around 150 patients a day across two branches.

**Users of the database:** Front-desk staff, doctors, laboratory scientists, pharmacists, billing officers and administrators.

**Major data stored:** Patients, doctors, departments, appointments, consultations and diagnoses, prescriptions and medications, lab test requests and results, invoices and payments.

**Key operations:** Register patients, book and cancel appointments, record diagnoses, issue prescriptions, record lab results, retrieve patient history, generate invoices, process payments, and produce reports.
